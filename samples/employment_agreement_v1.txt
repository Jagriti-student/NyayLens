FICTIONAL SAMPLE - EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT - VERSION 1

This fictional agreement is provided only to demonstrate the NyayLens document workspace. The people and company below are invented.

Parties: Northstar Labs and Asha Mehta.
Effective date: August 12, 2025.

Compensation: The Company will pay the Employee INR 20,000 per month on the last working day of each month.

Working hours: The Employee will work 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday.

Paid leave: The Employee may take 18 days of paid leave each year with reasonable prior notice.

Confidentiality: The Employee shall keep confidential all non-public business, customer, and technical information during and after employment.

Work product: Work product created in the course of employment belongs to Northstar Labs.

Termination: Either party may terminate this agreement by giving written notice.

Notice requirement: The Employee shall provide thirty days prior written notice before leaving the Company.

Company property: The Employee must return company property at the end of employment.
